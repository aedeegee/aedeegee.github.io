Data Science and AI Research Clusters

Our six research clusters span the full breadth of AI and data science research: from foundational principles of large language models (LLMs), transformers, and reinforcement learning, to human-centered questions in explainable AI, visual analytics, and AI in education; from privacy-preserving and trustworthy AI to high-performance graph analytics and big data systems; and on to application areas such as AI for health, genomics, scientific machine learning, and FinTech. Many faculty belong to more than one cluster, and each cluster below lists all of its affiliated faculty.

To PhD applicants: start with the questions that excite you, then reach out 1:1 to the people listed.

Applying to the PhD program

Admission is to the department, and you choose an advisor with their agreement. Naming the faculty you want to work with in your statement helps us route your application to the right readers.

See PhD program details
  1. Read the cluster descriptions and pick one or two that match your interests.
  2. Visit the homepages of two or three listed faculty and read a recent paper from each.
  3. Email them briefly: what you read, what question it raised for you, and when you plan to apply.
  4. In your statement of purpose, name those faculty and the cluster questions you want to pursue.

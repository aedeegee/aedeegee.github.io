Applying to the PhD program

Admission is to the department, and you choose an advisor with their agreement. Naming the faculty you want to work with in your statement helps us route your application to the right readers.

See PhD program details
  1. Read the cluster descriptions and pick one or two that match your interests.
  2. Visit the homepages of two or three listed faculty and read a recent paper from each.
  3. Email them briefly: what you read, what question it raised for you, and when you plan to apply.
  4. In your statement of purpose, name those faculty and the cluster questions you want to pursue.
